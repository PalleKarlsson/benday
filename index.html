Benday

Comics from your Komga server

A reader for Android phones and tablets. Point it at your Komga server and your whole library of comics, manga and webtoons comes along, online or offline.

AndroidGet it on Google Play How to get started

Free. Needs a Komga server you run or can sign in to.

Reading

Made for every kind of page

Western comics left to right, manga right to left, webtoons as one long strip. Benday picks the mode from the series, or you set it once per series.

Four reading modes

Left to right, right to left, vertical and webtoon. Spreads on tablets, with wide pages split or turned sideways on phones.

Webtoons that flow

Chapters chain into one continuous strip. Auto-scroll, side padding and a fast scroller for 300-chapter series.

Guided view

Panel by panel on a phone, so a full page reads at a comfortable size.

Your page, your way

Brightness, contrast, colour filters and sharpening, saved per series if you like.

Any way to turn

Tap zones, swipes, volume keys, keyboards and Bluetooth page turners.

Files on your device

Open CBZ, CBR and PDF files you already have, no server needed.

Your library

Everything on your server, arranged for you

Home is a set of shelves you can reorder: Continue Reading, On Deck, new books, Want to Read, the next books of series still running, and Discover, which suggests unread series like the ones you read and rate highly.

Libraries, collections and read lists all work as on the server, with search and filters, and two panes side by side on tablets. Progress, bookmarks, ratings and reader settings go through your Komga account, so every device picks up where the last one stopped.

Offline

Take it on the plane

Download single books, whole series or collections. Benday can keep the next few books of what you're reading downloaded, stick to Wi-Fi, and stay under a storage limit by clearing books you've finished.

No connection? Downloaded books open as usual, and your reading progress waits until you're back online.

Privacy

Nothing goes to us

Benday has no accounts, ads, analytics or crash reporting. It talks to your Komga server and to the services you connect yourself, and nothing else.
