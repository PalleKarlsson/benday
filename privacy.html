Privacy

Privacy policy

The short version: Benday sends nothing to us.

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Benday is a reader for comics, manga and webtoons stored on a Komga server that you run or have access to. Benday is developed by Palle Karlsson ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your data.

The short version

Benday does not send any data to us. It has no accounts with us, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. Your data stays on your device and on the servers you connect it to.

Data the app handles

Your Komga server address and sign-in. The server address, your username and your password or API key are stored on your device. Passwords, API keys and tracker tokens are kept in Android's encrypted storage. They are sent only to the server you entered, to sign you in.

Your library and reading. Covers, pages and books are loaded from your Komga server. Your reading progress is saved to your Komga account. Bookmarks, ratings, your Want to Read list and per-series reader settings are saved on your device and synced through your own Komga account, so they appear on your other devices. Downloaded books, image caches and reading statistics stay on your device.

Optional services you choose to connect:

  • MangaUpdates: if you sign in to MangaUpdates in Settings, Benday stores the session token on your device and sends your reading progress to MangaUpdates for the series you link. MangaUpdates' own privacy policy applies to that data.
  • Komf: if you enter the address of your own Komf server (a metadata tool for Komga), Benday sends it the series you ask it to identify.

Server certificates. If your server uses a self-signed certificate and you choose to trust it, Benday stores its fingerprint on your device so it can recognise the same server later.

Backups

Settings › Backup creates a file with your settings, server addresses, usernames, reading statistics and history. It does not include passwords. The file is saved where you choose; we never receive it.

Permissions

  • Internet: to reach your Komga server and any services you connect.
  • Notifications: to show download progress and new books in series you follow.
  • Foreground service (data sync): to keep downloads running while the app is in the background.
  • Biometrics: only if you turn on the app lock.
  • Read storage (Android 12 and older): to open comic files you pick from your device.
  • Write storage (Android 9 and older): only to save a page to your Pictures folder when you ask for it.

Beta testing

Joining the beta means joining a Google Group with your Google account, so that Google Play lets you install the test version. The group is run by Google under Google's terms; the developer sees the members' email addresses and uses them only to run the test. Leave the group to stop testing and be removed.

Children

Benday is not directed at children. It shows whatever content is on the servers you connect it to.

Deleting your data

Everything Benday stores is on your device or on your own servers. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it deletes the data on your device. Data on your Komga server is managed by that server; data sent to MangaUpdates is managed by MangaUpdates.

Changes
