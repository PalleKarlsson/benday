Beta testing

Read with Benday before everyone else

Benday is in a closed test on Google Play before it opens to everyone. Testers get the app for free and help shake out the last bugs.

Sign-ups open soon. The test starts as soon as Google has approved the developer account. Check back in a few days.

  1. Join the tester group with the Google account you use on Google Play.

    Join the tester group

  2. Accept the test on Google Play's test page (sign in with the same account).

    Open the test page

    The link follows here as soon as the first test build is out.

  3. Install Benday from Google Play as usual. As a tester you won't be charged.

What testing means

  • Use Benday with your Komga server, the way you'd read anyway: your own, or one a friend shares with you. No server yet? Komga is free, and the guide shows where to start.
  • Keep it installed for at least two weeks: Google requires a test of 14 days before an app can go public.
  • Tell us what breaks or bothers you on the feedback page, or with the Feedback button in Google Play.
  • Android 7.0 or newer, phone or tablet.

Free for testers

Benday will be a one-time purchase on Google Play. During the test, testers install it without paying.

Your address stays with Google. The tester group is a Google Group; only the developer sees who is in it, and it's used for nothing but the test. Leave the group to stop testing. See the privacy policy.
