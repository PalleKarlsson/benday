Getting started

Benday reads from Komga, a free server for comics, manga and webtoons that you install on a computer, NAS or home server. Benday doesn't come with any comics: it shows what's on your server.

  1. Have a Komga server. Your own, or one a friend shares with you. Follow Komga's installation guide and add a library.
  2. Install Benday from Google Play on your Android phone or tablet (Android 7 or newer).
  3. Sign in with the server's address and your Komga account. See Signing in.
  4. Start reading. Your libraries appear under Libraries; anything you've started shows on Home.

Signing in

Server address

Enter the address you use to open Komga in a browser, for example http://192.168.1.20:25600 on your home network or https://comics.example.com from anywhere. If Komga runs under a path (https://example.com/komga), include it.

To read away from home, the server must be reachable from outside: through a reverse proxy with HTTPS, or a VPN such as WireGuard or Tailscale. Downloaded books work without any connection.

Password or API key

Sign in with your Komga email and password, or switch the sign-in form to API key and paste a key instead (Komga 1.15 or newer). Create one in Komga under Account settings, or once signed in under Settings › API keys. An API key can be revoked on its own without changing your password, and it works for accounts that sign in through single sign-on.

HTTPS and self-signed certificates

Servers with a normal certificate (Let's Encrypt, for example) just work. If your server uses a self-signed certificate, Benday shows the certificate's details and fingerprint and asks whether to trust it. Only trust it if it's your server: Benday then remembers that certificate for that address and warns you if it ever changes.

Several servers

Settings › Servers keeps more than one server; switch between them there. Tracker links are kept per server.

Home and library

Home is a column of shelves: Continue Reading, On Deck (the next book of series you're reading), new books, Want to Read, Upcoming (the next book of series still running, with an estimated date) and Discover. Drag shelves to reorder or hide them. To add your own, filter a library and choose Save as Home shelf.

Updates (See all on the New Books shelf) lists new books by day and series and marks the ones added since you last looked; the filter button narrows it to series you're reading.

Libraries, Collections and Read Lists mirror your server, with search, filters (read status, genre, publisher, age rating and more) and sorting. On tablets, lists and details sit side by side.

Long-press a book or series for quick actions: mark read or unread, download, add to a collection or read list, or open the series.

Reading

Reading modes

ModeFor
Left to rightWestern comics
Right to leftManga
VerticalPage by page, scrolling down
WebtoonOne continuous strip; chapters follow each other without a break

Benday uses the series' reading direction from Komga, and guesses webtoon from very tall pages. Change it from the reader's toolbar for this series only, or set a default in Settings › Reader.

Turning pages

Tap the left or right edge, swipe, or use the volume keys (Settings › Reader › Tap zones and volume keys). Keyboards and Bluetooth page turners work too: arrow keys, Page Up/Down and space. With a Samsung S Pen, press the button for the next page, double-press for the previous one, or swipe the pen left or right (Air actions; remap them in Android Settings › Advanced features › S Pen). Tap the middle of the page to show the toolbar and the page slider.

Spreads and wide pages

In landscape, and on tablets, Benday can show two pages side by side. If a spread starts on the wrong page, shift it by one. In portrait, wide double pages can be split into two pages or turned sideways.

Guided view

Guided view moves panel by panel, which suits phones. Turn it on from the reader's toolbar. If it gets the panels of a page wrong, report it from the page's menu (long-press the page).

Bookmarks and page actions

Bookmark a page from the toolbar; all bookmarks of a book are listed in the bookmarks sheet. Long-press a page to save or share it, or (as a Komga admin) make it the book's or series' cover.

Display settings

The gear in the reader opens the display settings: page fit, zoom, background, brightness, contrast, saturation, colour filters, sharpening, and webtoon side padding and gaps. Choose whether changes apply to this series only or to every series; per-series settings follow you to your other devices.

Settings › Look changes the whole app's look: eight styles, each with a light and dark side, following the system's dark mode or set by hand.

Downloads and offline

  • Download a book, a whole series, a collection or a read list from its menu. Downloads continue in the background, with a progress notification.
  • Books to keep ready: Benday can keep the next books of the series you're reading downloaded, so the next one is always there.
  • Auto-download new books of a series when they arrive on the server.
  • Wi-Fi only and a storage limit (Settings › Downloads). Over the limit, the oldest books you've finished are removed first.
  • Simultaneous downloads: up to three books download at once (two by default).
  • SD card: on a device with a memory card, Downloads › Queue & settings › Download location keeps downloads on the card, and offers to move the ones you already have.
  • Downloads › Library shows everything on the device, grouped by series, even with no connection.

Reading progress made offline is saved on the device and sent to the server the next time you're connected.

Sync between devices

Benday keeps its shared data in your Komga account, so there is nothing extra to set up:

  • Reading progress (Komga's own, also seen by Komga's web reader and other apps)
  • Bookmarks, ratings and Want to Read
  • Per-series display settings and reading direction

The app's theme, look, image quality and smart downloads setting follow your account too. Downloads, statistics, history and the other settings stay on each device.

Ratings and Discover

Rate a series from its page. Ratings, together with what you've read, feed the Discover shelf, which suggests unread series that share writers, artists, genres or tags with the ones you liked, and says why. Want to Read is a list of series to start later.

Tracking and Komf

MangaUpdates: sign in under Settings › Tracking, link a series from its menu (Track on…), and Benday updates your MangaUpdates list as you read. If MangaUpdates is further along than Komga, Benday offers to mark the earlier books read.

Komf is a metadata tool for Komga. If you run one, enter its address in Settings; admins then get Identify with Komf and Auto-match in a series' menu.

Files on your device

The Local Files tab opens CBZ, CBR and PDF files (and ZIP or RAR archives of images) from your device without a server. Benday remembers where you stopped in each.

Privacy and app lock

  • App lock asks for your fingerprint, face or screen lock when Benday opens. It needs a screen lock on the device.
  • Incognito reads without recording progress or history, until you turn it off.
  • Benday sends nothing to its developer. See the privacy policy.

Backup and restore

Settings › Backup & restore saves a file with your settings, server addresses, usernames, statistics and history. It never contains passwords or API keys, so after restoring on a new phone you sign in again. Downloads aren't included.

Troubleshooting

"Cannot reach server"

Open the same address in the phone's browser. If that fails too, the phone can't reach the server: check that you're on the same network (or the VPN is on), the port is right, and there's no firewall in the way. localhost means the phone itself, so use the server's network address instead.

"This doesn't look like a Komga server"

The address answered, but not with Komga. A login page (an SSO proxy, for example) or a wrong path is in the way. Use the address that opens Komga itself.

Certificate errors

Check the certificate is valid for the name you typed. For a self-signed certificate, accept the trust dialog when Benday offers it. If the certificate changed (renewed), Benday asks again.

Something else

Look through the FAQ, or report it.
