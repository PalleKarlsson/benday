Feedback

Tell us what you think

Bugs, ideas and questions go on a public board, where you can also follow what happens to them and vote on other people's ideas.

The feedback board isn't open yet. It will be by the time Benday is on Google Play.

What is it about?
For a bug: the steps that lead to it. For an idea: what you'd use it for.

How it works

  1. Fill in the form. Nothing is sent from this page.
  2. The feedback board opens in a new tab with your text filled in.
  3. Sign in there (a free account) and press Create. You'll get replies by email.

Everything on the board is public. Leave out your server's address, usernames, passwords and anything else private.

Before posting, see whether it's already there: browse the board. A thumbs-up on an existing idea counts as much as a new post.

Quick questions are often answered in the FAQ.

Enjoying Benday? A rating on Google Play helps other Komga users find it.
